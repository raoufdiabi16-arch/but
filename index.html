    AIRVER · Policies

    Store policies

    Shipping, returns, privacy and terms of sale — everything that applies to your order, together in one place.

    1. 01Shipping & delivery
    2. 02Returns & exchanges
    3. 03Privacy & your data

    Section 01

    Shipping & delivery

    From our hands to your door, across Algeria.

    1.1Delivery

    We deliver to every wilaya in Algeria. Orders ship within 2–4 business days of confirmation. Choose home or office delivery at checkout.

    1.2Confirming your order

    We call the phone number on your order before it ships — this protects you from mistaken or fraudulent orders placed in your name.

    1.3Delivery cost

    The delivery fee depends on your wilaya and on whether you choose home or office delivery. It is shown in your total before you confirm — nothing extra is collected on arrival beyond that total.

    Section 02

    Returns & exchanges

    Clear, simple rules for exchanges and refunds.

    2.1Exchanges

    Wrong size? Message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. Exchanges are free as long as the item is unworn, unwashed, and still has its tags.

    2.2Refunds

    For cash-on-delivery orders, you can refuse the parcel at the door at no cost. For online payments, refunds for eligible returns go back to the original payment method.

    2.3Damaged or wrong item

    If your order arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll sort it out at our cost.

    Section 03

    Privacy & your data

    What we collect, why, and the rights you have over it.

    3.1What we collect

    Only what's needed to deliver your order: name, phone number, delivery address and wilaya, and your chosen size and payment method.

    3.2How it’s used

    To confirm and ship your order — nothing else. We don't sell or share your information with advertisers.

    3.3Where it’s stored

    Order data is stored securely with Supabase. Card details for online payments are entered directly on Chargily's secure payment page and never pass through our servers.

    3.4Your rights

    Under Algerian Law n° 18-07 on the protection of personal data, you can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information at any time — message us on WhatsApp.

    Section 04

    Terms of sale

    The simple terms behind every order.

    4.1Orders

    Prices are listed in Algerian dinars (DZD). An order is confirmed once we've reached you by phone.

    4.2Payment

    We accept cash on delivery and online payment by Edahabia or CIB card via Chargily's secure gateway.

    4.3Our right to cancel

    We may cancel and refund an order we can’t confirm by phone, or reasonably believe to be fraudulent.

    4.4Governing law

    These terms are governed by the laws of Algeria.
